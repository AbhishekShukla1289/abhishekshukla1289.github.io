about.md

Hi, I'm Abhishek.

backend · distributed systems · APIs

I'm a backend engineer at Yahoo, working on the services behind Yahoo Finance subscriptions, including the API that onboards new subscribers.

Before Yahoo I worked at FTNSS and TigerGraph, and spent three years at Oracle building banking APIs for more than 50 clients across Europe. I have an M.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Santa Clara University.

Most of my work sits where data moves between services: queues, retries, caches and the APIs in front of them. I like systems that stay correct when messages arrive twice or a dependency goes down, and I like load-testing them before users do.

Away from the keyboard I cook a lot, and I'm told I'm good at it.
